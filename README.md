# Ojos Locos: growing sales and EBITDA (CreditSwan, management edition 1.0)

DRAFT, built on Ojos's system data to FY2026 period 10 (period 11 booked, not closed). Prepared 20 Nov 2026.

One file, `index.html`: open it in a browser, or deploy the folder as a static site.

What is where
- 01 Overview: the story in one page per finding (a headline, one chart, three points), then the links to the detail.
- 02 Diagnosis, 03 Market, 04 Stores, 05 Revenue: where sales and store profit went.
- 06 Savings and 07 Overhead: about $7.8M a year of savings at the base case, and the ten quick hits.
- 08 Way forward: the workstreams, the weekly scorecard, the next 100 days and the questions by person.
- 09 Appendix: summary tables and management's cost actions.
- 10 Assumptions, 11 FAQ, 12 About CreditSwan.

Figures are from Ojos's Mirus, R365 and Toast systems and the documents shared; each exhibit names its source and basis.
